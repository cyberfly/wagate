- Messages and cached attachments are stored unencrypted in a local database. Anyone with access to your computer account can read them. Use full-disk encryption and a locked user account.
- WhatsApp sessions and your OpenRouter key are stored encrypted, with the key held in your operating system's credential store.
- If you turn on AI features, the content needed to write a draft or a post (recent messages of that chat, or your topics and earlier posts) is sent to OpenRouter and the model provider you chose, under their terms. Nothing is sent to them otherwise.
- If you start a public tunnel, anyone who holds one of your API keys can read your chats and send messages from the numbers that key can use.
